Study runner five-core third-party notices
===========================================

Scope
-----

This notice index describes the Pyodide 314.0.8 browser subset consisting of
`pyodide.mjs`, `pyodide.asm.mjs`, `pyodide.asm.wasm`, `python_stdlib.zip`, and
`pyodide-lock.json`. It does not include package payloads named by the lock
metadata, the Node/CLI files, or any unrelated application dependency.

The exact pinned upstream license, copyright, and source-header texts included
under `LICENSES/` are:

* Pyodide MPL-2.0; CPython PSF-2.0, the pinned `Doc/license.rst`
  incorporated-software acknowledgements, and README attribution context.
* Emscripten, emsdk, LLVM, Binaryen, musl, libc++, libc++abi, and libunwind
  license/copyright texts, plus the Emscripten bzip2 and zlib port licenses.
* HACL* Apache-2.0 license plus the generated-file MIT source header.
* libmpdec README context plus its BSD-style source header.
* Expat MIT, libffi MIT, Hiwire MPL-2.0, XZ/liblzma `COPYING`, zstd BSD, and
  SQLite's official public-domain copyright page.
* The Pyodide-vendored stackframe/error-stack-parser MIT notice and the
  Node-only `ws` 8.18.3 MIT notice used by the package's Node branch.

The source URLs, exact byte counts, and SHA-256 receipts for every included
text are in `SOURCE-AVAILABILITY.json`. The two source-header files are exact
prefixes containing the notices from the pinned CPython source files; their
full source-file URLs and full-source receipts are recorded in the manifest.

Proven asset identity
---------------------

The five members are from Pyodide 314.0.8, release commit
`f37a18559ae11c5f371beb1c9462a13a663fc461`. The member hashes in
`SOURCE-AVAILABILITY.json` bind the candidate assets. This is source and
artifact provenance; it is not by itself a browser, hosted-deployment, or
legal-release qualification.

Candidate build closure and attribution boundary
------------------------------------------------

The conservative candidate build closure is derived from the pinned Pyodide
and CPython build recipes and includes CPython, HACL*, libmpdec, Expat,
libffi, Hiwire, XZ/liblzma, zstd, SQLite, Emscripten bzip2, Emscripten zlib,
Emscripten, LLVM, Binaryen, musl, libc++, libc++abi, and libunwind. The pinned
link configuration also names `libstdc++`, GL/EGL/WebGL/HTML5/SDL libraries,
worker/websocket support, and `libjsverror`. Those named inputs remain
unresolved in this fixture: there is no per-input source identity or separate
notice mapping for them, and they are open source-bundle obligations rather
than claims of coverage.

The exact contribution of each named system library to this particular Wasm
file was not decomposed by a linker map in this preparation. This notice
therefore must not be read as claiming that every conservative candidate is
proven present, or that the list is an exhaustive binary-composition proof.
Conversely, the absence of a component from the five-file archive does not
erase its source/notice obligation if the final link uses it.

V8, the LLVM test suite, and Ninja are recorded separately as build/test
provenance. They are not asserted to be runtime members of the five-file
browser subset.

Source and digest policy
------------------------

`SOURCE-AVAILABILITY.json` distinguishes commit-pinned URLs, tag URLs,
versioned official URLs, and the one unversioned SQLite copyright-page
snapshot. The SQLite page bytes were retrieved on 2026-11-14 and are bound by
the recorded SHA-256; its page says it was last updated on 2026-01-12, so it
is not an immutable source URL. The Emscripten bzip2 1.0.6 and zlib 1.3.1
port recipes provide source URLs and recipe-declared SHA-512 values; their
source archives are not present here. XZ 5.2.2, zstd 1.5.7, and SQLite 3.39.0
have source identities but no upstream archive digest in the retained
metadata. No local archive digest is claimed for those sources until their
exact bytes are assembled into a source bundle; a future intake must compute
and retain local SHA-256 receipts.

The lock file is metadata only. Its 346 package and 11 shared-library records
do not mean that those payloads or their notices are distributed here. A
package-enabled profile requires a separate payload, license, notice, and
source review.

The stock Python 3.14.2 metadata observation advertises `_bz2`, `zlib`,
`_lzma`, `_zstd`, `_sqlite3`, `pyexpat`, `_ctypes`, `_decimal`, `_blake2`,
`_hmac`, `_md5`, `_sha1`, `_sha2`, and `_sha3`. The bzip2 and zlib entries are
covered here by the pinned Emscripten port recipes and exact license texts;
the metadata declaration is not treated as a complete linker-membership proof.

Stdlib member form and scan basis
---------------------------------

The retained `python_stdlib.zip` has 614 entries: 47 directories and 567
regular source/data files. Its regular members are 559 `.py`, one `.txt`, two
`.ini`, two `.typed`, one `.ctypes`, one extensionless file, and one `.bat`;
there are no `.pyc` members in this retained asset. The 133 marker-bearing
count is a bounded scan observation over the mapped source-member text using
selected copyright/license/SPDX marker families (the family counts overlap),
with 132 direct pinned CPython `Lib/` paths and one generated sysconfigdata
output. It is not a legal classifier and does not imply that the ZIP preserves
every source-tree notice omitted by the packaging filter.

JavaScript dependency inventory boundary
-----------------------------------------

The pinned `src/js/package.json` and `package-lock.json` receipts identify
`@types/emscripten` as type-only and `ws` 8.18.3 (MIT) as the only direct
runtime package dependency. The package's browser map disables `ws` and the
Node builtins. A literal import scan of the exact retained browser members
finds no external browser static or dynamic package import; `pyodide.mjs`
contains Node-only imports (`node:url`, `node:fs`, `node:fs/promises`,
`node:vm`, `node:path`, `node:crypto`, `ws`, `node:child_process`), while
`pyodide.asm.mjs` also contains Node-only glue imports. The emitted browser
member contains the pinned vendored stackframe/error-stack-parser source, whose
MIT notice is included. Development-only lock records and any package
payloads are outside this five-file notice set; no claim is made for them.

No legal sufficiency, public-release approval, complete linker attribution,
package closure, or hosted-origin qualification is claimed by this fixture.
