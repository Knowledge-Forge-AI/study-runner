# Contributing to Study Runner

Study Runner accepts contributions only under the project terms in
[CLA.md](CLA.md). Contributors retain copyright and grant the steward the
copyright and patent rights described there, including rights to distribute
under AGPL-3.0-or-later and commercial licenses.

Submit only original work or material for which you have authority. Identify
third-party source, generated output, and notices before submission. Preserve
the authoritative terms for Pyodide, CPython, and every other distributed
component; Study Runner's AGPL license does not relicense those materials.

Every pull request must include this exact acknowledgement in its body:

```text
I agree to the Study Runner contribution terms in CLA.md.
```

Also confirm that you have authority to submit the work under `CLA.md`.
Questions about contribution terms can be sent to sam.lair@knowledge-forge.ai.
